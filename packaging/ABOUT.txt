Flatwater
=========

Flatwater: Open-source recreations of Waves mixing plug-ins, null-tested against the originals.
Open source under the GNU Affero General Public License v3: https://github.com/MaymiPR/Flatwater

It is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Waves Audio or Chris Lord-Alge.
Product names belong to their owners and only describe what each plug-in is modelled on. None of their code, artwork,
preset files, fonts or other files is included: each plug-in is built from measurements of how the original sounds
and behaves, and its editor is drawn by Flatwater.
