Flatwater for Windows (64-bit)
===================================

Run Flatwater-<version>-Windows-Setup.exe. It installs every Flatwater plug-in:
  - VST3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Flatwater
  - CLAP to C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP\Flatwater
  - optionally the standalone apps to C:\Program Files\Flatwater
Pick "Choose plug-ins and formats" to leave some out, then rescan plug-ins in your DAW.
Uninstall from Settings > Apps > Flatwater.

A signed installer opens without a warning. An unsigned one (the release notes say which) makes Windows SmartScreen
warn: click "More info", then "Run anyway".
To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt on the Releases page:
  certutil -hashfile Flatwater-<version>-Windows-Setup.exe SHA256
