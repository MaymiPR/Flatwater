Flatwater for Linux (64-bit x86, built on Ubuntu 22.04)
==========================================================

In this folder run:   sh install-linux.sh
It copies every Flatwater plug-in to ~/.vst3/Flatwater and ~/.clap/Flatwater, and the standalone
apps (the Standalone folder, when present) to ~/.local/bin. Then rescan plug-ins in your DAW (Reaper, Bitwig,
Ardour...). Remove them again with:  sh install-linux.sh --uninstall

Needs glibc 2.35 or later, X11 (XWayland works), libasound2 and libfreetype6.
To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt on the Releases page:
  sha256sum Flatwater-<version>-Linux.tar.gz
