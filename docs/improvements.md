# Open items

What each Flatwater plug-in does not yet match, and what would be needed to close it. The learned-block procedure the
suite uses is [learned-blocks.md](learned-blocks.md); each plug-in's MEASUREMENTS.md holds the measurements behind
an item.

| Plug-in | Item | What would be needed |
|---|---|---|
| Flatwater Vocal Comp | Real vocals are not null-tested: every voice signal is synthetic (harmonic tone, syllable envelopes, formants) | nulls against the reference on real dry vocal stems at typical settings (Compression -6 to -24) |
| Flatwater Vocal Comp | Compression automated while audio plays is not in the training data (one fixed setting per clip) | clips with Compression ramps and steps, their null, and those clips in training if needed |
| Flatwater Vocal Comp | AM at light compression is worse with the learned path (-59 to -49 dB at Comp -12); level steps at Comp -6 and -12 move from about -100 to -80 ... -88 dB; inaudible | a targeted fine-tune on those cases |
| Flatwater Vocal Comp | Dense material at the heaviest settings is close but not a null (noise -24 to -33 dB, voice about -30 dB at Comp -36); how the slow path forms its live level over a few milliseconds is not identified | the slow path's structure; untried: a longer training run with more data, a larger network, input features from the gate |
| Flatwater Vocal Chain | Compress's learned correction: at +6 ... +10 on loud unseen vocals Push and Wall reach about -29 dB, Spank -27 ... -28; Wall is worse at steps -7, -6 and 0 on a few vocals (up to +1.5 dB) in every training, a bias | more Wall material at those steps, or an input the net lacks there (the de-esser's gain, the colour filter's output) |
| Flatwater Vocal Chain | The nets' cost (two per colour, about 3 % of a core): the GRU step is plain loops | SIMD for the two matrix products, or one net trained to match the pair |
| Flatwater Vocal Chain | The doubler's Wide and Spreader splices part from the reference on dense material; Wide at 44.1 kHz picks a neighbouring step | the reference's step decision on dense signals |
| Flatwater Vocal Chain | The main de-esser's stereo link and its reading of loud noise | probes of the link with unequal channels and of noise against tones |
| Flatwater BassX Lift | The learned level control is about 8 dB short of the structure's ceiling (random grid: -28 dB median against -37 dB with the reference's own extracted gain; `plugins/FwBassXLift/nulltest/null_oracle_grid.py`); more data, more units and long fine-tunes gave a few dB each; envelope-follower inputs and feeding back the output gave nothing; an explicit two-follower detector with a learned gain law (`plugins/FwBassXLift/nulltest/train_net_det.py`) was 8 dB worse than the GRU (-30.8 against -39.1 dB on the validation set) | a supervised pretraining stage on constrained extracted gains (`plugins/FwBassXLift/nulltest/extract_g3.py`) mixed with the output loss |
| Flatwater BassX Lift | Kicks (-19 dB on the harmonics) are close to what the structure reaches with the reference's own gain (-21 ... -28 dB); the rest is spread over all bands, part of it the reference's monitor clipping at +-1 on the first peak | none within the structure |
| Flatwater BassX Lift | Extreme settings (strong Decay, high Ratio, loud low bass near Freq): the reference's Decay loop turns chaotic (Nyquist oscillation); Flatwater BassX Lift goes unstable under the same condition and stays bounded (y within +-1) | not reproducible sample by sample |
| Flatwater BassX Lift | Real material (bass stems, full mixes) is not null-tested; every signal is synthetic | nulls on real stems |
| Flatwater Bass Lift | Weak spots: Freq 256 (created harmonics about -17 dB median), kick-like transients (about -22 dB), Freq 160 with Original Bass Out on very low input (30 Hz tone -4 dB) | more training renders of those cases |
| Flatwater Bass Lift | Original Bass meter: after a long steady tone stops, the reference's bar sometimes snaps down at once instead of easing (the same on Flatwater Vocal Comp's output meter); rules tried in `plugins/FwVocalComp/nulltest/fit_meter_output.py`; not visible on music | the rule behind the snap |
| Flatwater Comp | Release law and level detection: still weakest on low notes under long attacks (bass at Attack 30 ... 60 ms: -15 ... -20 dB), Release 300 on voice (-22 dB), fast attacks on drums (-23 dB); the reference's level detection averages across low cycles in a way the held-peak structure does not | identifying that averaging (helps most); more training data around those settings (`plugins/FwComp/nulltest/render_trainset.py`, then retrain with the steady-tone guard and the no-worse penalty) |
| Flatwater Comp | Limiter: the reference's limiter sees the true future output; Flatwater Comp estimates it (-16 ... -43 dB under heavy limiting) | the reference's limiter structure |
| Flatwater Comp | VU print: drawn about a centre about 0.4 px from the printed arc's; the needle's edges a little harder than the reference's (within 0.1 in darkness) | a finer centre and edge model |
| Flatwater Comp | Analog mode 3: nulls -29 ... -64 dB (median -40) | a finer table near the transition (0.02 ... 1) |
| Flatwater Delay | Glide tails: in a glide's slow tail the interpolator's 1/32 phase changes on different samples than the reference's (-40 ... -80 dB on broadband material while it settles); neither double nor float32 variants of the update reproduce it | the reference's exact glide arithmetic |
| Flatwater Delay | The interpolator's window is not quite Kaiser (up to 4e-4); LoFi shows it (-70 dB) | a clean measurement of the window at every phase (the sweep during a glide is too smeared) |
| Flatwater Delay | Self-oscillating loops with the filters (Feedback > 100 % with HiPass or LoPass, the preset Quick Alarm) are chaotic and null only for their first fraction of a second | a bit-exact loop (the reference's exact float operations in the filters and the gain stage) |
| Flatwater Delay | 96 kHz: the kernel's band above 20 kHz (round its 24 kHz cutoff) differs from the reference's; below 20 kHz the delay path nulls at -46 ... -61 dB, high-Feedback loops at -31 ... -44 dB | the 96 kHz kernel measured on its own |
| Flatwater Delay | Analog modes 1 to 3: -40 ... -56 dB including their noise; mode 3 the weakest (-40 dB): its low-level response below 1 kHz is off by -18 ... -40 dB and depends on the level (still nonlinear at -60 dBFS), so neither more fitting of the same structure nor a correction EQ fixes it | a learned block trained on mode 3's renders at low levels and low frequencies |
| Flatwater Bass Leveler | Drops onto lower notes at Response Fast: a held note at 100 Hz (the 2048-sample window) followed by a quieter one at 50 ... 78 Hz (the 4096-sample window) is ridden afresh once the drop is deep enough (10 dB at 78 Hz, 14 at 69, 20 at 50 and 62), the gain always moving +3.0 ... 3.3 dB; from 55 Hz never; most of the worst preset's error (Fast, Artifacts Ignored on the bass line, -9.9 dB) | a rule for these re-rides |
| Flatwater Bass Leveler | 90 Hz (and 85 ... 88 Hz at some rates and Sensitivities) re-rides 30 ... 90 ms after a release, +0.7 ... 3 dB | the same |
| Flatwater Bass Leveler | Overlapping notes: when the louder of two ringing notes stops, the quieter one left is ridden afresh if it is lower in pitch, not if higher; partly covered by the band rule (39 ... 47 Hz) | the same |
| Flatwater Bass Leveler | Re-rides on real bass that the reference takes and the model does not (at onsets and inside notes): most of the corpus error at Fast | the probes above as leads |
| Flatwater Bass Leveler | The second re-ride in a long 42 Hz decay comes 20 ... 40 % sooner in the reference (its slow envelope seems to hold after a re-ride) | a probe of the slow envelope after a re-ride |
| Flatwater Bass Leveler | The end of a fading note: about 42 dB under its peak in the reference, 38 in the model (about 60 ms later on plain decays) | the end rule |
| Flatwater Bass Leveler | Before the first note, silence is read on the level read E (-77.7 dB); the reference's own measure ignores a -54 dBFS 3 kHz tone, which E does not (a band-limited level, probably the detector's low-passed input) | the detector's input band |
| Flatwater Bass Leveler | The lock after an attack (when the gain stops following): a learned block on the corpus gave no clear win (`plugins/FwBassLeveler/nulltest/fit_lock.py`; the bass line lost at Fast) | the lock's rule |
| Flatwater Vocal Leveler | The Music side-chain: the reference uses the music only while its host reports the side-chain active, which no offline host here does; the plug-in rides without it | a host with a real side-chain send |
| Flatwater Pitch Bend | The depth curve of a modulated Pitch (at most about 20 cents off with ours linear); the scorecard's last 3 level cells; the reference's level dips and ridges at single grid points | a law that fits every depth case; level probes at those points |
| Flatwater Pitch Bend | PT on speech: on a phrase's falling, creaky end the reference's PT goes 150 ... 500 cents lower | the reference detector's call on rough voice |
| Flatwater Pitch Bend | Where a shifted output's grains sit after a reset (at Pitch +5 the reference's first onset 152 samples, then 184 ... 262; ours 102 throughout) | the reference's first-grain rule after silence |
| Flatwater Pitch Bend | Noise when a knob is set and its total is back at 0: the reference re-granulates to +2 ... +3 dB | a decision whether to copy it (the +1.8 dB at Pitch +-1 is not copied) |
| Flatwater Pitch Bend | Undo after Default Preset brings back the parameters but not the slots, shapes and Fine | the editor-only settings in the undo state |
