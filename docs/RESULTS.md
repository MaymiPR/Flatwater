# Null-test results

How closely each Flatwater plug-in matches the plug-in it is modelled on. The residual is
`20 log10(rms(ours - reference) / rms(reference))` in dB: lower is better, about -60 dB or less is a close match,
0 dB means nothing in common. Numbers are reported as measured; no case is called a null unless the data shows it.
Each plug-in's NULLTESTS.md is generated by its null-test script (named in each section) and holds every case; this
page summarises them. How each model was found is in each plug-in's MEASUREMENTS.md.

## Flatwater Pitch Bend

`plugins/FwPitchBend/nulltest/null_presets.py`, `plugins/FwPitchBend/nulltest/null_mods.py`, `plugins/FwPitchBend/nulltest/score_shifter.py`:

- With every control at 0 both pass the voice through with the same delay: -67 dB on a sung take, the delay within
  0.0002 samples.
- Once anything shifts, Flatwater Pitch Bend is its own pitch shifter (a pitch detector and overlap-added grains), so
  it is judged by what is heard: the shifter meets all but one of its scorecard measures against the reference's
  numbers (pitch accuracy 0.4 cents on vowels, 6.3 on sung phrases against the reference's 6.0; the formant envelope;
  onsets and jumps within 5 ms; Flatten; the gate); the miss is a few level cells where the reference's own level
  varies by more than 1 dB within one setting.
- The modulators (LFOs, sequencers, the AM and PT followers, and modulation of each other's controls), read through
  Mix: 38 of 38 cases within 0.003 to 0.01 of the control's range.
- The reference's 40 presets against ours (Flatwater names), on a sung and a spoken take: 62 of 80 pairs within the
  level, pitch and tone targets; the misses are mostly speech, where two pitch detectors part on rough voice, blends
  at about 35 % Mix, and presets that use the original's drawn LFO shapes, which are not shipped.
- Sample nulls with a shift: on a steady vowel the grains sit where the reference's do (-13 to -27 dB with small
  Formant moves); on a real voice the reference rebuilds the sound from its own period estimates, so no recreation
  nulls it sample for sample.
- Details: [NULLTESTS.md](../plugins/FwPitchBend/NULLTESTS.md), [MEASUREMENTS.md](../plugins/FwPitchBend/MEASUREMENTS.md).

## Flatwater Vocal Chain

`plugins/FwVocalChain/nulltest/nulltest.py`, held-out vocal parts at -6 dBFS peaks, the Noise off in both:

- Bass, Treble and Reverb stages with the others cleared (55 cases): median -75.7 dB at 48 kHz, worst -67.9 dB;
  -77.7 dB at 44.1 kHz and -71.0 dB at 96 kHz.
- The whole chain at the defaults, factory presets and random settings: median -33.2 dB at 48 kHz with the doubler
  (the Pitch stage) clear, -32.1 dB at 44.1 kHz and -35.5 dB at 96 kHz. With the doubler on its default Stereo
  colour: -36.0 dB at 48 kHz, -35.6 dB at 44.1 kHz, -18.1 dB at 96 kHz.
- The doubler's Wide and Spreader colours are pitch shifters that pick their splice points from the signal, so a
  voice parts from the reference wherever the two pick different steps: medians -9 to -24 dB with them on, worst
  -3.4. They are compared in behaviour instead: level within 0.05 dB, third-octave spectrum within 0.12 dB, and
  similar delay ranges and movement cycles.
- Compress uses a small learned correction on top of the identified compressor; it is the weakest of the other
  stages, about -24 dB on the most extreme presets.
- The reference's own 43 presets, loaded into it, against Flatwater Vocal Chain's (Flatwater names): median -30.1 dB,
  -31.2 dB with the Pitch stage cleared ([NULLTESTS-presets.md](../plugins/FwVocalChain/NULLTESTS-presets.md)).
- Details: [NULLTESTS.md](../plugins/FwVocalChain/NULLTESTS.md), [MEASUREMENTS.md](../plugins/FwVocalChain/MEASUREMENTS.md).

## Flatwater Vocal Leveler

`plugins/FwVocalLeveler/nulltest/nulltest.py`: -74 dB on level steps and -47 ... -66 dB on vocal parts held out from the
identification, at Studio and Live, Slow and Fast, other Targets, ranges and Idle values, Stereo, and at 44.1 and
96 kHz. Attack Fast is the weakest (about -50 dB), at word onsets.

| Set | Result |
|---|---|
| Sample rates (held-out vocal parts) | 44.1 kHz -63.5 dB, 96 kHz (Fast) -53.2 dB |
| Components | Stereo -60.3 dB, Live -60.4 / -46.6 dB (Slow / Fast) |
| The reference's own 13 presets, loaded into it, against ours (three held-out vocal parts) | median -60.9 dB, worst -37.9 ([NULLTESTS-presets.md](../plugins/FwVocalLeveler/NULLTESTS-presets.md)) |

- How: the reference's riding was identified from probes and a corpus of 36 vocal parts (a DAW's own vocal phrases).
  It rides only on the samples its vocal detector passes, which is why it rises slowly on a real voice; every
  constant is the reference's own hidden value, none is fitted.
- Not covered: the Music side-chain (Music Sensitivity). The reference uses the music only when its host reports the
  side-chain active, which no offline host here does; Flatwater Vocal Leveler rides without it.
- Details: [NULLTESTS.md](../plugins/FwVocalLeveler/NULLTESTS.md), [MEASUREMENTS.md](../plugins/FwVocalLeveler/MEASUREMENTS.md).

## Flatwater Bass Leveler

`plugins/FwBassLeveler/nulltest/nulltest.py`. The level read, the gain law, the 42 ms look-ahead, the smoothing and the hold
null at -48 ... -49 dB on steady tones and level steps; a bass line at -30 dB at nearly every Sensitivity x Response
setting (-25 ... -28 dB at Sensitivity 75 and 100), at 44.1 and 96 kHz alike.

| Set | Result |
|---|---|
| Sample rates (the bass line) | 44.1 kHz -30.2 dB, 96 kHz -30.0 dB |
| Components | Live Mono -26.0 dB (Spill -25: -21.2, Spill -15: -24.2), Stereo (different bass on L and R) -27.7, Live Stereo -26.8 |
| Real bass parts held out from the fit | median -22 ... -26 dB per setting, the worst parts -10 ... -17 dB |
| The reference's own 17 presets, set on both (bass line and two corpus parts) | median -24.8 dB (-25.2 in [NULLTESTS-presets.md](../plugins/FwBassLeveler/NULLTESTS-presets.md)'s set); Live components -26 dB, Stereo -27 dB |

- Nearly all of the error on real bass parts (a DAW's own bass samples played as bass lines) is a note ridden afresh
  by one side and not the other, after which the two hold 1 ... 4 dB apart until the next note; in a mix that is one
  note slightly louder or quieter than with the reference, not an artefact. It is weakest at Response Fast and
  Sensitivity 100.
- How: the reference's note detector (a windowed note level by pitch band, slow and long envelopes, follow and hold,
  release and re-attack rules, the Idle glide) was identified from about 1,200 probe signals and its riding on a
  corpus of 36 bass parts; its constants are fitted, and some rules (which notes are ridden afresh at Fast) are
  matched to what the probes show rather than known.
- Details: [NULLTESTS.md](../plugins/FwBassLeveler/NULLTESTS.md), [MEASUREMENTS.md](../plugins/FwBassLeveler/MEASUREMENTS.md).

## Flatwater Delay

`plugins/FwDelay/nulltest/nulltest.py`. The delay path nulls: defaults, ms and synced times, the filters, Ping Pong from
either side and Feedback up to 200 % at -84 ... -104 dB; modulation at -61 ... -81 dB; glides inside the input
-60 ... -83 dB.

| Set | Result |
|---|---|
| Sample rates | 44.1 kHz as 48 kHz; 96 kHz below 20 kHz -46 ... -61 dB on the delay path (the band round the 24 kHz kernel cutoff differs) |
| The reference's own 88 presets, loaded as it loads them (drums, bass and voice-like, each with its own Analog mode) | median -53.2 dB; Analog Off -78.1, mode 3 -41.2; Quick Alarm chaotic (self-oscillation through the filters) |

- Analog modes: mode 4 nulls at -80 ... -90 dB; modes 1 to 3 add random noise as the reference does, which no null can
  remove (-53 / -51 / -40 dB with their noise), and mode 3's model is the weakest part (about -41 dB).
- Limits: the slow tail of a glide (-40 ... -80 dB while it settles), LoFi (-70 dB), and Feedback above 100 % with the
  filters on, where the loop self-oscillates chaotically (the preset Quick Alarm): such a loop nulls only for its first
  fraction of a second, in any model short of a bit-exact one.
- How: the reference's interpolator (a 32-phase Kaiser sinc), its tape glide, the gain stage in the loop, its float
  triangle LFO, the filters, Ping Pong's routing and every parameter's resolution were identified on the reference by
  nulling; the Analog modes are fitted models (mode 4 is Flatwater Comp's).
- Details: [NULLTESTS.md](../plugins/FwDelay/NULLTESTS.md), [NULLTESTS-presets.md](../plugins/FwDelay/NULLTESTS-presets.md),
  [MEASUREMENTS.md](../plugins/FwDelay/MEASUREMENTS.md).

## Flatwater Comp

`plugins/FwComp/nulltest/null_fwcomp.py`, 14 signals x 12 settings (the default, fast and slow releases, heavy ratios, Mix, the
output limiter, the four Analog modes): median -48.7 dB, worst -22.1 dB; the reference's 26 factory presets (each on
voice, drums, bass and pink noise): median -44.0 dB, worst -20.8 dB.

- At the default settings: tones and sweeps -84 dB (a null), voice-like -61 dB, bass line -57 dB, white noise -43 dB,
  drums -33 dB, pink noise -32 dB.
- Weakest: low notes under long attacks (30 ... 60 ms: -15 ... -20 dB on a bass line), a long release on voice (300 ms
  at threshold -24: -22 dB), fast attacks on drums (about -23 dB), the output limiter on voice (-24.5 ... -28 dB), and
  a few presets on drums (Hard Squash -20.8 dB, Light Kit -26.4, Parallel Blend, Heavy Drums and Pumping Kit -29 ...
  -30).
- The learned correction: the null test from -41.4 dB median (worst -16.8) without it to -47.0 (worst -24.5) over 168
  cases (-37.2 / -9.6 before the CMA-ES refit of the release law), 70 cases better and one worse by more than 1 dB;
  the presets from -35.6 to -43.7 dB median (Analog Off, from 0.5 s, a fresh reference per preset), 82 better and 4
  worse by more than 1 dB (all still -58 ... -72).
- Analog modes: mode 4 nulls at -82 ... -100 dB on loud material; modes 1 to 3 add random noise as the reference does,
  which no null can remove (mode 2, the default, is limited to about -54 ... -69 dB by its noise alone).
- How: the detector, static curve, makeup, attack, smoothing, mix, limiter, tempo sync and the Analog stage were
  identified on the reference; the program-dependent release is a fitted two-state law (its tables found by CMA-ES on
  1034 measured cases), not the reference's algorithm, and a small learned correction (a GRU over Flatwater Comp's own
  states, trained on 4300 renders of the reference) fixes most of what that structure misses: low notes under long
  attacks, long releases, deep compression. Two reference quirks: a slow start-up after a reset (modelled) and a
  hidden synced release time that survives switching Sync back to ms (not copied: reload the reference before nulling
  after a synced preset). The VU needle and limiter lamp follow the reference's, filmed from its editor's OpenGL
  frames.
- Details: [NULLTESTS.md](../plugins/FwComp/NULLTESTS.md), [NULLTESTS-presets.md](../plugins/FwComp/NULLTESTS-presets.md),
  [MEASUREMENTS.md](../plugins/FwComp/MEASUREMENTS.md).

## Flatwater BassX Lift

`plugins/FwBassXLift/nulltest/nulltest.py`, 12 signals x 8 settings (Freq, Ratio, Response, Decay, High-pass, the Original
Bass and Harmonics faders): median -48.5 dB on the whole output and -42.7 dB on the created harmonics alone.

- Per signal (created harmonics, median over the settings): white noise and quiet tones -49 to -59 dB; tones, sweeps
  and pink noise -40 to -49 dB; level steps -34 dB; bass notes -27 dB; kick-like transients -19 dB.
- Over 120 random settings of every control with the hardest signals (bass notes, kicks, a loud tone, pink noise):
  median -35.2 dB on the whole output, -29.2 dB on the harmonics (`plugins/FwBassXLift/nulltest/null_grid.py`; 480 cases).
- Worst cases: loud material at extreme settings (Freq 256 with Ratio 4 and Decay -12, or Decay -9), where the
  reference's own harmonic feedback goes unstable.
- How: the filters, the harmonic generator with its Decay feedback, the faders, monitors and clip were identified
  exactly; the harmonics compressor (Ratio, Response) is a small recurrent network (48 units, about 7 % of one core)
  trained on renders of the reference, so it is a learned model of that part, not its algorithm.
- Details: [NULLTESTS.md](../plugins/FwBassXLift/NULLTESTS.md), [MEASUREMENTS.md](../plugins/FwBassXLift/MEASUREMENTS.md).

## Flatwater Bass Lift

`plugins/FwBassLift/nulltest/null_fwbasslift.py`, 12 signals x 8 settings (Freq, Intensity, Gain, Original Bass In / Out): median
-36.8 dB on the whole output and -31.5 dB on the created harmonics alone.

- Per signal (median over the settings): tones at or above Freq, sweeps, white noise -43 to -49 dB; bass notes, level
  steps, pink noise and tones below Freq -31 to -40 dB; kick-like transients -28 dB.
- Worst cases: Freq 160 with Original Bass Out on very low input (30 Hz tone -4 dB, kicks -9 dB, 60 Hz tone -16 dB):
  the output is then only the created bass, and its level is off there.
- How: the filters and the harmonic generator were identified exactly; the gain control of the created bass is a small
  recurrent network (32 units, about 6 % of one core) trained on renders of the reference, so it is a learned model of
  that part, not its algorithm. Meters move like the reference's (measured from its editor).
- Details: [NULLTESTS.md](../plugins/FwBassLift/NULLTESTS.md), [MEASUREMENTS.md](../plugins/FwBassLift/MEASUREMENTS.md).
