# Flatwater: Research

What each original documents and shows in a host, one file per plug-in, listed below. Nothing of the originals is
copied into this repository.

## Naming

| Original (Waves) | Flatwater plug-in | Product folder |
|---|---|---|
| Renaissance Vox | Flatwater Vocal Comp | Flatwater Vocal Comp |
| Renaissance Bass | Flatwater Bass Lift | Flatwater Bass Lift |
| MaxxBass | Flatwater BassX Lift | Flatwater BassX Lift |
| H-Comp | Flatwater Comp | Flatwater Comp |
| H-Delay | Flatwater Delay | Flatwater Delay |
| Bass Rider | Flatwater Bass Leveler | Flatwater Bass Leveler |
| Vocal Rider | Flatwater Vocal Leveler | Flatwater Vocal Leveler |
| CLA Vocals | Flatwater Vocal Chain | Flatwater Vocal Chain |
| Vocal Bender | Flatwater Pitch Bend | Flatwater Pitch Bend |

An original's mono, stereo, mono-to-stereo and live components are one plug-in with a Mode switch.
